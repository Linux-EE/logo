# Linux-EE Logo

## Why was this logo designed?

I designed this logo because I believe it better represents Linux-EE and that bluish colors are more suitable for Linux-EE. My goal is to create a unique design identity for Linux-EE.

## Why am I sharing this as open source?

Because we believe people should have access to just about everything related to the Linux distributions they use.

## What is permitted?

* You may use the logo in your personal projects and in non-commercial website or blog posts.
* You may not use this logo in any commercial materials.
* You may use it to reference Linux-EE.

## What tool was used to make it?

* [PhotoCraft](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft)
